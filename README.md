# Introduction

My journey into technology started back in 2014 on Tumblr, where I would customise blog themes using HTML and CSS. I had no idea I was coding at the time; I was just a teenager experimenting and learning through trial and error.

Years later, after graduating from UCL, I came across code again and was reminded of those early experiences. I decided to explore that interest further through The Odin Project, a free, open-source web development course.

As I continued learning about technology, I discovered cybersecurity and became particularly interested in how organisations protect their systems, data and users. I'm now working towards a career in cybersecurity, building on my legal background while developing practical technical skills.
